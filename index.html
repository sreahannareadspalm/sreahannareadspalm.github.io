Most people book when something is on their mind. It might be a relationship, a job offer or a move to another city. I go through your hands one line at a time and explain what I'm looking at, so you can follow along and ask questions as we go. I won't tell you your fate is fixed. You'll leave with a clearer idea of your own patterns, and what you do with that is up to you.

What a reading looks like

A complete reading takes about an hour. I start with the overall shape of your hands and the mounts below each finger, then move on to the main lines and the smaller marks.

If you come with a particular question, tell me at the start. I'll keep coming back to it as we go.

Had a reading with me? I'd like to hear how it went. Send me a review on WhatsApp.

Before you book

If your question isn't here, ask me on WhatsApp.

How does an online reading work?
After you book, send me two clear photos of each palm taken in daylight, plus one of the side of each hand. Then we talk on a video call. If a call doesn't suit you, I can do the reading from the photos alone.
Which hand do you read?
Both. Traditionally your non-dominant hand shows the temperament you were born with, and your dominant hand shows what you've done with it. Most of what's interesting comes from comparing the two.
Where in Bangalore do we meet?
I'll send you the address on WhatsApp when I confirm your booking.
Which languages do you read in?
English, Hindi, Malayalam and Kannada. Choose one when you book.
Can you tell me exactly what will happen in my future?
No, and I'd be wary of anyone who says they can. Palmistry shows tendencies and patterns, which can help you think through decisions. It isn't a substitute for medical, legal or financial advice.
How do I pay?
I'll send payment details on WhatsApp once your slot is confirmed.
